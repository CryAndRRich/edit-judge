You are preparing an automatic photo edit. A user posted this photo with an edit request.

Request (simplified): "$instruction"
Request (as written): "$request"
Type of edit: $operation

Answer with one JSON object, as described for the type of edit below. Coordinates are pixel coordinates of the image
you see. Every phrase is a short English noun phrase that names a thing as it looks in the photo ("the red car",
"the man's shirt", "the sky"), so that an object detector can find it.

- recolor: one region of the photo gets a new colour. Answer
  {"targets": [{"phrase": "...", "bbox_2d": [x1, y1, x2, y2]}, ...], "prompt": "..."}
  "targets" are the things whose colour changes (at most 5), each with its box. "prompt" describes those things
  in their new colour ("a bright red sports car", "a navy blue shirt"). If the request changes the colour or tone
  of the whole photo (black and white, warmer, sepia, a filter), "targets" is [] and "prompt" describes the result.

- format: the request changes the format of the photo, not its content. Answer
  {"action": "...", "box": [x1, y1, x2, y2] or null, "degrees": number or null, "direction": "..." or null,
   "scale": number or null, "prompt": "..."}
  "action" is one of:
  - "crop": keep only a part of the photo; "box" is the part to keep;
  - "rotate": "degrees" counter-clockwise (90, 180, 270, or a small angle such as -3 to straighten it);
  - "flip": "direction" is "horizontal" or "vertical";
  - "resize": make the photo larger or smaller; "scale" is the factor (2 for "twice as big", 0.5 for half);
  - "extend": make the canvas larger and fill the new part (outpainting); "direction" is "left", "right", "top",
    "bottom", "horizontal", "vertical" or "all"; "scale" is the new size relative to the old one (default 1.5);
    "prompt" describes the whole scene after extending;
  - "grayscale": make the photo black and white;
  - "cutout": cut the subject out of its background; "prompt" names the subject;
  - "other": anything else.
  Use null for the fields the action does not use, and "" for an unused prompt.

- cutout: the subject is cut out of its background. Answer {"action": "cutout", "prompt": "..."}, where "prompt"
  names the subject and the background the request asks for ("the dog on a plain white background").

Answer with the JSON object only.

{"targets": [{"phrase": "...", "bbox_2d": [x1, y1, x2, y2]}, ...],
 "keep": [{"phrase": "...", "bbox_2d": [x1, y1, x2, y2]}, ...],

Coordinates are pixel coordinates of the image you see. Every phrase is a short English noun phrase that names a
thing as it looks in the photo ("the man on the left", "the white text", "the cat's head"), so that an object
detector can find it, with that thing's box. Use empty lists and null for the fields the type does not use.

- remove: "targets" are the things that must disappear (at most 5). "prompt" is "".
- replace: "targets" are the things to be replaced (at most 5). "prompt" describes what is painted in their place
  ("a red sports car", "a black leather jacket").
- background: the background is repainted. "keep" are ALL the subjects that must stay unchanged: every person,
  animal and object in front that the request does not ask to change (read the request: "leaving the people and
  the sign" means keep the people and the sign; things it asks to remove are not kept). "prompt" describes the
  new background ("a galaxy with stars and nebulae").
- add: the new thing is painted into a region.
  - If the new thing goes on or at something already in the photo (facial hair on a face, a hat on a head, a
    tattoo on an arm, a cup in a hand), "anchor" is that thing; the region is around it. Otherwise "anchor" is
    null and "bbox_2d" is the region where the new thing goes, the size it should have, in an empty part of the
    photo (behind or beside the subjects, not over them).
  - "keep" are the people, animals or objects near that region that must not be painted over (for "add a camel
    behind us": the people). Never list the anchor itself in "keep".
  - "prompt" describes the whole region after the edit ("a man's face with a full black beard", "a camel standing
    on the beach").

Answer with the JSON object only.
